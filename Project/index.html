⚡ Core Controls & Features

Wiring:

Click & drag from any Output pin (circle on the right) and connect to an Input pin (left). One output can drive multiple inputs; an input accepts one wire.

Toggling Inputs:

Click directly on any Switch on the canvas to toggle between 0 (Low) and 1 (High). Signals propagate instantly!

Accessibility Design:

High (1) is represented by thick glowing lines with animated electron particles, filled glowing bulbs, and [1] tags. Low (0) is thin, dimmed, and hollow [0].

Wire Clean-up:

Select any gate and press Del or click the trash icon. All connected wires are automatically cleaned up with no dangling connections!
